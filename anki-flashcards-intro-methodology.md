## 🧠 Master Mnemonic Toolkit (Quick Reference)

1. **The 4-Fold Theological Plot:** Creation (*Image*) -> Fall (*Wound*) -> Redemption (*Christ*) -> Glory (*Theosis*).
2. **Quid vs. Quis:** *Quid* = Quality/Species (*What animal?*); *Quis* = Question of the Subject (*Who stands before God?*).
3. **Boethius vs. Aquinas on Person:** Boethius gives the *Building block* (*Substantia*); Aquinas gives the *Act of existing* (*Subsistens*).
4. **Tzelem vs. Demuth:** *Tzelem* is *Tangible* (statue); *Demuth* is *Diffuse* (spiritual likeness).
5. **Irenaeus' Eikōn vs. Homoiōsis:** *Eikōn* = *Endures* (rational nature); *Homoiōsis* = *Holiness* (robe of grace lost & restored).
6. **The 135 Leap (*Magnifica Humanitas*):** Leo XIII (1891, *Rerum Novarum*) protected against *factories*; Leo XIV (2026, *Magnifica Humanitas*) protects against *algorithms*.
7. **Pelagius' P-V-E Triad:** *Posse* is *Provided* by God in nature; *Velle* & *Esse* are *Vanishingly Exclusively* man's.
8. **Sanans vs. Elevans:** *Sanans* *Stitches* the wound; *Elevans* *Escalates* to the Trinity.
9. **Carthage vs. Orange II:** *Carthage* = *Can't* do good without grace (*ut diligatur et fiat*); *Orange* = *Origin* of faith is grace (*initium fidei*).
10. **The Christian J-A-R (Rémi Brague):** Secondary dependence on *Jerusalem* (faith), *Athens* (reason), and *Rome* (law).
11. **Bara vs. Yatsar:** *Bara* = 100% God alone (55 times, *creatio ex nihilo*); *Yatsar* = Potter molding clay.
12. **Christ is Not Napoleon:** External copycat fails; need *divine life and grace infused* within.
13. **Ezer = Eye-to-Eye:** Woman stands face-to-face with man as God-like saving partner (*kenegdo*), not subservient servant.
14. **Ish & Ishshah Echo:** Man discovers relational sexed identity only when standing before woman (*unitas duorum*).
15. **Z-N Complementarity:** *Zakar* (initiator) and *Neqebah* (receiver), reflecting the Trinitarian communion.
16. **Shepherd, Not Bulldozer:** Biblical dominion (*radah*) is pastoral custody, not Babel's technocratic exploitation.
17. **Blessing Before the Bite:** Fertility and sexuality precede the Fall; procreation is God's original blessing.
18. **Mater Unitatis & Magnificat Optics:** Mary perceives God's victory not despite fragility, but through it.
19. **Hebrew Psychology (Nefesh, Ruach, Leb):** *Nefesh* = Neck/throat (vitality); *Ruach* = Rushing Wind (spirit); *Leb* = Cockpit of reason & will.
20. **Caro Cardo Salutis & Huiothesia:** Flesh is the hinge of salvation; *Huiothesia* places us into the Son's seat at the Father's table.
21. **O-M-S-E (Dignitas Infinita):** Ontological (permanent), Moral (free choices), Social (conditions), Existential (subjective).
22. **Sirach's Sensorial Five:** Eyes, ears, tongue, heart, counsel—given to praise and keep the Law.
23. **Wisdom's Eternity:** Image = Incorruption (*Aphtharsia*); death entered through the devil's envy.
24. **Kingu's Blood vs. Peaceful Dabar:** Babylon makes man a slave from rebel blood; God makes man a royal son by His word.
25. **Plato's Escape vs. Christian Resurrection:** Plato seeks likeness by fleeing the body; Christ redeems the body.
26. **Christ in the Clay (Tertullian):** *Quodcumque limo exprimebatur, Christus cogitabatur homo futurus*.
27. **The Father's Two Hands (Irenaeus):** Son models the clay, Spirit breathes divine life.
28. **Eikōn Stays, Homoiōsis Flies:** Fallen man keeps the rational image; Christ reinstalls the lost likeness.
29. **Recapitulation (Anakephalaiōsis):** Christ rewinds Adam's fall and replays it in perfect obedience.
30. **Mirror vs. Sun:** Christ is the Image; man is made *ad imaginem* to be conformed to the Firstborn.

## 📇 The 75 High-Yield Atomic Cards

### Card 01 — What is the formal object of Theological Anthropol...
- **Front:** What is the formal object of Theological Anthropology (Antropologia Teologica)?
- **Back:** The human being considered in the light of divine Revelation: created in the image of God (imago Dei), wounded by sin, redeemed in Christ, and called to supernatural deification (theosis) and eternal communion with the Triune God.
- 🧠 **Mnemonic:** Remember the 4-fold Plot: **Creation (Image) -> Fall (Wound) -> Redemption (Christ) -> Glory (Theosis)**.

---

### Card 02 — What is the speculative distinction between 'quid ...
- **Front:** What is the speculative distinction between 'quid sit homo' and 'quis sit homo'?
- **Back:** <b>Quid sit homo (Nature/Essence):</b> what man is as a rational animal (animal rationale), studied by biology, cosmology, and philosophy.<br><b>Quis sit homo (Person/Subject):</b> the unique, incommunicable subject (incommunicabilis existentia) addressed by God by name and called into an I-Thou filial covenant.
- 🧠 **Mnemonic:** <b>Quid</b> = <b>Qu</b>ality of the species (What animal?); <b>Quis</b> = <b>Qu</b>estion of the Subject (Who stands before God?).

---

### Card 03 — What is Boethius' classical definition of the pers...
- **Front:** What is Boethius' classical definition of the person (De duabus naturis)?
- **Back:** <i>"Persona est rationalis naturae individua substantia"</i> (An individual substance of a rational nature).
- 🧠 **Mnemonic:** <b>B-R-I-S:</b> <b>B</b>oethius = <b>R</b>ationalis <b>I</b>ndividua <b>S</b>ubstantia.

### Card 04 — How does St. Thomas Aquinas refine Boethius' defin...
- **Front:** How does St. Thomas Aquinas refine Boethius' definition of the person (ST I, q. 29, a. 3)?
- **Back:** <i>"Persona significat id quod est perfectissimum in tota natura, scilicet subsistens in rationali natura"</i> (A subsistent individual in a rational nature).
- 🧠 **Mnemonic:** <b>B</b>oethius gives the <b>B</b>uilding block (<i>Substantia</i>); <b>A</b>quinas gives the <b>A</b>ct of existing (<i>Subsistens</i>: perfection of Esse).

### Card 05 — What Hebrew terms in Genesis 1:26 designate the 'i...

- 🧠 **Mnemonic:** <b>T</b>zelem is <b>T</b>angible (concrete statue); <b>D</b>emuth is <b>D</b>iffuse (spiritual resemblance).

### Card 06 — What distinction did St. Irenaeus of Lyons establi...
- **Front:** What distinction did St. Irenaeus of Lyons establish between 'image' (eikōn) and 'likeness' (homoiōsis)?
- **Back:** <b>Image (eikōn):</b> the natural, indelible constitution of man (rationality, free will, immortality of the soul) which remains after the Fall.<br><b>Likeness (homoiōsis):</b> the supernatural robe of the Holy Spirit (grace, original justice) lost by Adam's sin and restored by Christ.
- 🧠 **Mnemonic:** <b>E</b>ikōn = <b>E</b>ndures (human nature never erased); <b>H</b>omoiōsis = <b>H</b>oliness (supernatural robe lost and restored).

## 🌿 Protology & Imago Dei Cards (Gen 1–3, 5, 9)

### Card 36 — What are the two principal Pentateuchal traditions...
- **Front:** What are the two principal Pentateuchal traditions of creation in Genesis?
- **Back:** 1) <b>Priestly Tradition (P) - Gen 1:1–2:4a:</b> cosmic, liturgical hymn over 7 days, creation by God's Word (Dabar), culminating in the Sabbath;<br>2) <b>Yahwist Tradition (J) - Gen 2:4b–25:</b> dramatic, earthly narrative, molding man from dust (adamah) and divine inbreathing (neshamah), Eden, loneliness, and creation of woman (tsela).
- 🧠 **Mnemonic:** <b>P</b> is <b>P</b>riestly & <b>P</b>omp (Liturgical Hymn); <b>J</b> is <b>J</b>ahwist & <b>J</b>ourney (Earthly Clay & Drama).

### Card 37 — What does the Hebrew verb 'Bara' (בָּרָא) uniquely...
- **Front:** What does the Hebrew verb 'Bara' (בָּרָא) uniquely signify in the Old Testament?
- **Back:** Absolute divine production without pre-existing materials (creatio ex nihilo). In the entire Hebrew Bible, <b>Bara has strictly God as its subject</b>. In Genesis 1:27, it is climactically repeated <b>three times</b> for the creation of man.
- 🧠 **Mnemonic:** <b>Bara = Belongs to God Alone!</b> Repeated 3x for man: Crown of creation.

### Card 38 — What is the difference between 'Bara' (בָּרָא) and...
- **Front:** What is the difference between 'Bara' (בָּרָא) and 'Yatsar' (יָצַר) / 'Asah' (עָשָׂה)?
- **Back:** <b>Bara:</b> sovereign divine creation ex nihilo without materials.<br><b>Yatsar / Asah:</b> fashioning, molding, or making from pre-existing matter (like a potter molding clay, Gen 2:7); can have human artisans as subjects.
- 🧠 **Mnemonic:** <b>Bara</b> = From Nothing; <b>Yatsar</b> = Potter's Clay.

### Card 39 — What was the royal meaning of 'Tzelem' (צֶלֶם) in ...
- **Front:** What was the royal meaning of 'Tzelem' (צֶלֶם) in the Ancient Near East and its Biblical democratization?
- **Back:** In the Ancient Near East (Egypt/Mesopotamia), a <i>tzelem</i> was a physical statue representing the King in distant provinces; only the King/Pharaoh was the 'image of god.' Genesis 1 democratizes this royal status: <b>every single human individual</b> is the living viceroy and statue of God.
- 🧠 **Mnemonic:** <b>Tzelem = The King's Statue:</b> Genesis tears down the Pharaoh's monopoly and crowns every human as God's statue.

---

### Card 40 — What is the theological function of 'Demuth' (דְּמ...
- **Front:** What is the theological function of 'Demuth' (דְּמוּת) alongside 'Tzelem' in Gen 1:26?
- **Back:** It acts as a <b>safeguard of transcendence</b>. Derived from <i>damah</i> (to resemble), it indicates that man is an image, but only according to an analogical likeness, preventing crude physical anthropomorphism and pantheistic confusion.
- 🧠 **Mnemonic:** <b>Demuth = Distance & Difference:</b> Man resembles God, but is NOT God.

---

### Card 41 — What is the theological significance of the divine...
- **Front:** What is the theological significance of the divine plural 'Faciamus hominem' (Gen 1:26)?
- **Back:** God departs from the 3rd person command (Fiat lux) and engages in a solemn deliberation. Patristic and Thomistic theology sees here the <b>internal council of the Holy Trinity</b>: the Father creating man through the Son in the Holy Spirit.
- 🧠 **Mnemonic:** <b>Faciamus = Family Council:</b> The Triune Persons confer before molding man.

---

### Card 42 — What does the Hebrew wordplay between 'Adam' (אָדָ...
